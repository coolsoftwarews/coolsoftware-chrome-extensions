Catch the posts asking for exactly what you sell.

Three things to know, then you're done reading this page forever.

  1. Open a group you're already in and add a rule — or pick a starter pack in the panel. A rule needs two parts: an intent phrase ("looking for") and a topic word ("bookkeeper"). A post only counts when it has both. 💡 opportunity — "looking for" + "bookkeeper"
  2. Scroll the feed as you normally would. Matching posts get a small badge — nothing else on the page changes, and nothing is clicked, liked or commented on for you.
  3. Open the panel to see every matched post — group, author, text, a link back to it — mark it replied once you've reached out, and export the list as CSV or Markdown any time.

No alerts, on purpose

This only reads what Facebook renders while you're actively looking at a group. Background scanning would mean automation, and automation is what gets accounts and extensions banned. The habit that works instead: open the panel when you open Facebook.

No account. No cloud. No network.

Group content is personal, so it stays that way: every rule and every matched post lives in this browser's local storage and is never uploaded. Export everything from the panel's Data menu any time, or clear it all with one click.
