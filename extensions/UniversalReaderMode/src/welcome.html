Strip the page. Keep the article.

Two things to know, then you're done reading this page forever.

  1. Open any article and click the toolbar icon (or press Alt+Shift+R). Reader mode strips the ads, nav and sidebars and shows just the writing — adjustable font, line width and a light/dark/sepia theme.
  2. Export it as Markdown, PDF or plain text before you close the tab. That's the whole point: the browser's own reader view disappears when you leave the page — this one leaves you with a file.

No account. No cloud. No network.

Nothing you read or export is uploaded anywhere — this extension makes no network requests at all, which you're welcome to verify in devtools. Reading preferences (font, theme, width) are saved locally so they carry over to the next article; no page content is ever stored.

Why it asks for access to all sites

A reader mode that only works on a list of approved sites isn't a reader mode. That permission is what lets extraction run on whatever article you happen to be reading — nothing leaves your machine because of it, and nothing runs on a page until you click the icon.

When it says "this doesn't look like an article"

Reader mode only works on article-shaped pages. Rather than guess and hand you a garbled mess on a dashboard, search page or app screen, it says so plainly and leaves the page alone.

Tip: Alt+Shift+R toggles reader mode open and closed on the current page.
