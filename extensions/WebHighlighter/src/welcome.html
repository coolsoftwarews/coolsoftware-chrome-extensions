Highlight anything. Take it with you.

Three things to know, then you're done reading this page forever.

  1. Select text on any page. A small toolbar appears — pick a colour.
  2. Click a highlight to change its colour, attach a note, or remove it. Your marks are still there when you come back tomorrow.
  3. Click the toolbar icon to open the panel, then copy the page as Markdown or export it as .md, .html, .pdf or .txt.

Try it on a real article →

No account. No cloud. No network.

Your highlights and notes live in this browser's local storage and are never uploaded — this extension makes no network requests at all, which you're welcome to verify in devtools. The flip side: they are on this device only. Use Data → Export all data in the panel to keep a backup, and Import to bring it to another machine.

Why it asks for access to all sites

A highlighter that only works on a list of approved sites isn't a highlighter. The permission is what lets the extension put your marks back on whatever you're reading — nothing leaves your machine because of it.

Tip: Alt+Shift+H opens the panel.
