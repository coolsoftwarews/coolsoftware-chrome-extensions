Stick a note on any page. It'll be there next time.

Three things to know, then you're done reading this page forever.

  1. Click the toolbar icon (or press Alt+Shift+N) on any page to drop a note where you're looking.
    Remember to fix this →
  2. Drag it by its header to move it, resize from the bottom-right corner, and pick a colour.
  3. Open the side panel (press Alt+Shift+P, or pick it from Chrome's side panel icon) to search every note you've left across every page, jump straight to one, or export them all as Markdown.

No account. No cloud. No network.

Your notes live in this browser's local storage and are never uploaded — this extension makes no network requests at all, which you're welcome to verify in devtools. The flip side: they are on this device only. Use the panel's Data → Export all data to keep a backup, and Import to bring it to another machine.

Why it asks for access to all sites

A sticky note tool that only works on a list of approved sites isn't a sticky note tool. The permission is what lets the extension put your notes back on whatever you're looking at — nothing leaves your machine because of it.

A note's position drifts a little if a page's layout changes a lot — its text never does.
