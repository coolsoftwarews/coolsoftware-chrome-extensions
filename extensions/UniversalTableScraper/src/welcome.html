Table/List → CSV is installed

Open any page with a table or a grid of cards, then:

1
Click the extension icon, then Pick a table or list.
2
Hover the page — a green outline shows what you're about to select.
3
Click it. A preview of the rows and columns appears in the corner of the page.
4
Export CSV or JSON. That's the whole product.
No account, no cloud, no server. Nothing you pick is ever sent anywhere — the file is built and saved on this device. Esc cancels picking mode at any time.
