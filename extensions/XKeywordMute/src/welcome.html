Mute the words you never want to see on X again.

Three things to know, then you're done reading this page forever.

  1. Add a rule — or enable a starter pack. A rule is a single keyword or phrase, matched case-insensitively by default. Choose substring, whole word, or (for power users) a regex pattern.
  2. Scroll your timeline as normal. A matching post collapses to one line — Hidden by filter: "crypto airdrop" — right where it was. Nothing is deleted, and nothing disappears without a trace. Click Show anyway any time.
  3. Open the panel to see every rule's hit count — the number that tells you whether a rule is doing anything, or hiding far more than you expected — and export your whole rule list as a single JSON file any time.

A hidden post is never a silent one

Keyword matching is never perfectly accurate — a rule that's too broad can hide things you actually wanted to see. That's why every hidden post stays visible as a labeled placeholder, one click from being shown again, and why every rule shows its own hit count instead of quietly working (or misfiring) in the background.

No account. No cloud. No network.

Your rule list lives in this browser's local storage and is never uploaded. Starter packs are bundled with the extension, not fetched from a server. Export everything from the panel's Data menu any time, or clear it all with one click.

Click the toolbar icon on x.com to open the panel.
